## 5. Website Page Structure & Navigation

**Format:** single-page scroll site with a sticky top nav (anchor links) — works natively as one GitHub Pages `index.html`, one long Notion page, or one Wix page with anchor menu.

```
NAV (sticky):  Summary · About · Skills · Experience · Projects · Deliverables · Certifications · Contact

01  Home / Hero          — name, role line, branding statement, CTA (Download Résumé / Contact Me), quick-stat "Delivery Ledger" card
02  Professional Summary — 3-paragraph summary, rewritten for recruiters
03  About Me             — narrative bio + quick-fact list (location, current role, certs, MBA)
04  Core Competencies     — scannable tag/chip grid
05  Technical Skills      — grouped by category (PMO tools, Guidewire, DevOps, DB/programming, AI/collab)
06  Domain Expertise      — 3 cards: P&C Insurance, Healthcare, Infrastructure/DevOps
07  Work Experience       — timeline, most recent first, achievement-oriented bullets
08  Key Projects           — 3 project cards with scope + outcome + tags
09  BA Deliverables        — 8-card grid: BRD, FRD, SOW, Impact Analysis, User Stories/Use Cases, RTM, UAT, Process Flows
10  Certifications         — badge/stamp cards
11  Achievements           — 6-card highlight grid, outcome-first phrasing
12  Education              — MBA + B.Tech
13  Résumé Download        — one-click PDF download button
14  Contact Me             — email, phone, LinkedIn, location
```

**For Notion:** replicate as a single page with Heading-2 blocks per numbered section and a table-of-contents block at the top (Notion auto-generates the anchor nav).
**For Wix/Squarespace:** the numbered sections map directly to Wix "Strip" sections; use the anchor-menu feature for the same sticky nav behavior.
**For GitHub Pages:** the delivered `index.html` deploys as-is — just add it to a repo's `main` branch and enable Pages.

## 9. Skill Gaps & Recommended Certifications

Your resume is strong on Agile/PMO execution and Guidewire domain exposure, but light on formal BA credentialing and advanced analytics depth — both are quick wins recruiters specifically screen for at the "Business Analyst" title level (vs. "Coordinator"):

1. **IIBA CCBA / CBAP** (or ECBA if starting out) — the most recognized standalone Business Analysis credential; directly supports a title jump from Coordinator to Business Analyst.
2. **Guidewire-specific credential** (e.g., a Guidewire Certified Associate track, if accessible) — since 3 of your biggest projects are Guidewire, a vendor-recognized cert would strongly differentiate you from generic BA candidates.
3. **CSPO (Certified Scrum Product Owner)** — complements your Scrum Master cert and signals you can own a backlog, not just facilitate ceremonies.
4. **Advanced Power BI / DAX certification** (Microsoft PL-300) — you already build dashboards; a formal credential converts that into a provable, recruiter-searchable skill.
5. **ITIL Foundation** — useful given your CI/CD/release-readiness work; signals IT service management literacy to infrastructure-heavy employers.
6. **Advanced SQL certification** — you list SQL as a skill; a credential (or a portfolio query sample) makes it verifiable rather than a bare bullet point.

Complete the in-progress PMP once you hit the eligibility hours — it's the single highest-leverage credential on your list for the Senior/PMO Analyst tier.

## 10. How to Stand Out from Other BA Candidates

1. **Publish a sanitized deliverable sample.** Attach one redacted BRD or process-flow diagram (no client-confidential data) as a downloadable PDF in the Deliverables section — most BA portfolios only *describe* artifacts; almost none *show* one.
2. **Add a short "before/after" case study.** Pick the GLOBUS or Guidewire program and write 150 words: the problem, your approach, the outcome. Recruiters skim resumes but read case studies.
3. **Quantify at least 3 achievements with real numbers** before you publish — even directional ranges ("cut post-release defects by roughly a third") outperform unquantified claims in recruiter screening.
4. **Record a 60–90 second intro video** for the Home section — Guidewire/insurance hiring managers often fill niche roles fast, and a video is a low-effort way to stand out in a stack of static resumes.
5. **Add short testimonial quotes** from a manager or stakeholder (even one or two lines) — third-party validation is rare in junior BA portfolios and carries outsized credibility.
6. **Keep the résumé PDF and the LinkedIn headline in sync** with this site's keyword set — recruiters cross-check profile-to-resume consistency, and mismatches read as red flags.
